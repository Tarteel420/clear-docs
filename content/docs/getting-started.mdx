## Intended audience

Clear is primarily designed for organizations and professionals in the IT Asset Disposition (ITAD) industry who need reliable, auditable, and compliant data sanitization for retired or redeployed IT equipment, including:

- ITAD technicians
- System operators
- IT administrators
- Compliance or audit personnel

## System prerequisites

### Hardware requirements

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Processor | Quad-Core CPU with concurrency and processing load |
| RAM | 8 GB or more recommended for environments running 10+ simultaneous wipe operations |
| Storage | 50 GB or more. SSD recommended for improved logging and reporting performance |

<Callout type="info">
Systems handling large batches of devices, or high levels of concurrent wiping, may require additional resources.
</Callout>

### Supported operating systems

Clear is optimized for **Linux**, specifically **Ubuntu 22.04 LTS (Jammy Jellyfish) or newer**.

<Callout type="warn">
Kernel version 5.4 or higher is required to ensure full NVMe Sanitize command support.
</Callout>

### Software dependencies

Administrator privileges are required on the system to install and operate Clear.
