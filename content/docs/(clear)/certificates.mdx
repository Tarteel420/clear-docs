Clear provides comprehensive reporting and certification capabilities to support compliance, audit requirements, and customer delivery. These outputs serve as verifiable proof that data sanitization was successfully completed.

Certificates and reports are generated automatically as part of the wipe process, and can be accessed, viewed, and exported as needed.

## Device Erasure Certificate

A Device Erasure Certificate is generated for each processed device upon completion of the wipe operation. It's typically used for:

- Compliance and regulatory requirements
- Customer delivery and proof of erasure
- Internal record-keeping and audits

Each certificate includes:

- Report Information
- Company Information
- System Information
- S.M.A.R.T Properties and Attributes
- Erasure Details (method, configuration, duration)
- Erasure Results (status, verification outcome)

## Generating a Device Erasure Certificate

<Steps>
<Step>Navigate to the **Search** tab.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Device Serial Number**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Search Device**. The certificate displays on the right side of the dialog.</Step>
<Step>Use the navigation icons to move between certificate pages.</Step>
<Step>Click the download icon. A save dialog appears.</Step>
<Step>Choose a location and click **Save**.</Step>
<Step>
Open the downloaded report.

![Device erasure certificate report](/docs-images/certificate-report.png)
</Step>
</Steps>
