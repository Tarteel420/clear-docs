The **Jobs** tab is the central workspace for managing and reviewing all sanitization sessions in Clear. Each wipe session is organized as a Job, so operators can monitor, control, and audit batch processing activities.

Within Jobs, operators can:

- Track batch-level progress across multiple devices wiped in parallel.
- Manage job states during execution (edit, terminate, or close jobs).
- View job-level results and monitor overall progress.
- Export consolidated reports and certificates upon completion.
- Maintain a complete, auditable history of all sanitization activities.

## Creating a wipe job

A wipe job is created when one or more devices are selected and a wipe operation is initiated.

<Steps>
<Step>
In the **Local Devices** tab, select one or more devices marked **Ready**. Review device information and status before adding a device to the list.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm any locked devices have been configured and unlocked first (see [Per Device Configuration](/docs/local-devices#per-device-configuration)).

![Selecting devices before wipe](/docs-images/select-devices-wipe.png)

<Callout type="error" title="Important">
Wiping is irreversible once initiated and permanently removes all data from the selected devices. Ensure the correct devices are selected and any required data has been backed up before proceeding.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Wipe Devices** from the top bar. The Select Job Confirmation dialog opens.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose **Create New Job** to start a new job and specify a **Job name** (required) and **Vendor** (optional):

![Create new job dialog](/docs-images/create-new-job.png)

Or choose **Add to Existing Job** and select an existing job to add the devices to:

![Add to existing job dialog](/docs-images/add-to-existing-job.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Yes, Wipe Devices** to start. The selected devices are grouped into a single job and move to the Jobs tab for monitoring.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Managing jobs

The Jobs tab gives visibility into active and completed jobs, so you can monitor progress, manage states, and access results. Devices from a vendor can be grouped into one job so reports and certificates for the whole batch can be generated together, then the job closed once all devices finish.

Click the **Jobs** tab in the navigation panel to access this workspace.

## Viewing job details

<Steps>
<Step>Click the **Jobs** tab.</Step>
<Step>
Click on the required job from the list. The Job Details view opens, showing all devices assigned to the job.

![Job details view](/docs-images/job-details-view.png)
</Step>
<Step>
From here you can review and edit job details, verify included devices, review device-level status, abort the job, or export job data for closed/completed jobs.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Edit job information

<Steps>
<Step>Click the **Jobs** tab — the jobs list displays.</Step>
<Step>
Locate the job, click the three dots in the top right, and select **Edit**.

![Edit job menu](/docs-images/edit-job-menu.png)
</Step>
<Step>
The Edit Job Details dialog opens.

![Edit job details dialog](/docs-images/edit-job-dialog.png)
</Step>
<Step>Update the **Job name** (required) and/or **Vendor** (optional).</Step>
<Step>Click **Save Changes**.</Step>
</Steps>

## Abort job

Abort a job to stop all ongoing sanitization processes — for example, if the wrong devices were selected, or an operational issue requires immediate termination.

<Callout type="warn" title="Note that...">
- Only available when the job is **Running**.
- Aborting terminates all in-progress device operations.
- Device status and its sanitization certificate will be terminated.
- The job status updates to **Aborted**.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>
Locate the job, click the three dots, then click **Abort**.

![Abort job menu](/docs-images/abort-job-menu.png)
</Step>
<Step>
A warning displays.

![Abort job warning](/docs-images/abort-job-warning.png)
</Step>
<Step>Click **Abort Job** to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

## Close job

Close a job to finalize it once all sanitization activities are complete and verified. This signals processing is done and no further changes are expected.

<Callout type="warn" title="Note that...">
- Closing a job moves it to the Closed category.
- No additional devices can be added once closed.
- The job can no longer be edited.
- It remains available for reporting, certificate generation, and audit review.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>
Locate the job, click the three dots, then select **Close**.

![Close job menu](/docs-images/close-job-menu.png)
</Step>
<Step>
A warning displays.

![Close job warning](/docs-images/close-job-warning.png)
</Step>
<Step>Click **Close Job** to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

## Filter jobs by status

Use the **Filter by Status** drop-down in the top-right corner of the Jobs workspace.

![Filter by status dropdown](/docs-images/filter-by-status.png)

**Active Jobs** — currently open and manageable. A job in this state can be:

- **Running** — one or more devices currently being wiped.
- **Completed** — all devices finished, but the job stays open for review, reporting, or export.
- **Aborted** — stopped by the user; in-progress operations terminated.

Active jobs are editable and can transition to Closed once processing and verification are complete.

**Closed Jobs** — finalized and read-only; retained for reporting, certificate generation, and audit purposes.

## Exporting job information

<Callout type="info">
Export actions are available only when jobs are **Completed** or **Aborted**.
</Callout>

### Export to file (CSV)

Exports job and device data as a CSV file. Selecting multiple jobs generates one consolidated file.

<Callout type="idea">
By default, all available columns are included. Customize which columns export in [Export Configuration](/docs/configuration-options#export-configuration).
</Callout>

Select the required job(s) and click **Export to File**.

![Export to file button](/docs-images/export-to-file.png)

Save the file to your desired location.

### Download certificates

For Completed or Aborted jobs, download certificates and reports as a compressed ZIP archive.

![Download certificates button](/docs-images/download-certificates.png)

Select the required job(s), click **Download Certificates**, and save to your desired location.

### Export to StockWise

Export jobs directly to StockWise. Select the required jobs and click **Export to StockWise**. Devices that pass sanitization can be exported as resale-ready inventory, along with their reporting data. See [Export to StockWise](/docs/configuration-options#export-to-stockwise) to set up the integration first.
