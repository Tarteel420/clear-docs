The **System Wipes** page lists every system wipe in your organisation, across all projects.

## Reviewing system wipes

<Steps>
<Step>Click **System Wipes** under **Overview** in the portal menu.</Step>
<Step>
Choose a result tab to filter the list: **All**, **Active**, **Stale**, **Pass**, or **Fail**. Each tab shows how many wipes it contains.

![System Wipes page](/docs-images/clear-portal/system-wipes.png)
</Step>
<Step>Search by **hostname or technician**, or set a date range with **From** and **To**.</Step>
<Step>
Review the results. Each row shows **System**, **CPU**, **RAM**, **Drives**, **Result**, and **Completed** (the date and time, or **In Progress**).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Exporting system wipes

Click **Export CSV** in the top right to export the system wipes as a CSV file. The columns included are set on the [Export Columns](/docs/clear-portal/export-columns) page.
