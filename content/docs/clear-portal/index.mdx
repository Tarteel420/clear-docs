<Callout type="info" title="Clear Portal is a separate product">
Clear Portal is a web-based management console. It is separate from the Clear desktop application documented under the **Clear** tab.
</Callout>

Welcome to the **Clear Portal User Guide**. This section explains how to monitor wipe activity and set up the projects, vendors, and integrations your organisation works with.

## Getting started

Your journey with Clear starts in Clear Portal. Follow these steps in order:

<Steps>
<Step>
**Receive your account.** Our team creates your Clear Portal account for you. You don't need to sign up.
</Step>
<Step>
**Log in to Clear Portal.** After you log in, the **Dashboard** opens.
</Step>
<Step>
**Download the Clear Desktop Application.** On the Dashboard, click the **Download** icon in the top-right corner, next to your account avatar. The Clear Desktop Application build downloads to your computer.

![Clear Portal Dashboard with the Download icon highlighted](/docs-images/clear-portal/getting-started-dashboard.png)
</Step>
<Step>
**Install the Clear Desktop Application.** Use the downloaded build and follow the steps in [Installation and Setup](/docs/installation) under the **Clear** tab.
</Step>
<Step>
**Activate a licence.** Download a licence file from Clear Portal and install it in the desktop application. See [Licences](/docs/clear-portal/licences).
</Step>
</Steps>

## What is Clear Portal?

Clear Portal gives your organisation one place to:

- See users, licences, and wipe session results at a glance on the **Dashboard**.
- Review projects, system wipes, and directly wiped drives, and export them as CSV.
- Download certificates for a project.
- Create and manage **projects** and **vendor profiles**, including their wiping configuration.
- Connect an **external system** (such as AccountWise), a **label printer**, and choose which **export columns** appear in CSV files.

## Portal navigation

The portal menu on the left is organised into three groups. The organisation you are working in is shown at the bottom of the menu.

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Dashboard, Projects, System Wipes |
| **Configuration** | Projects, Vendor Profiles, External System, Label Printer, Export Columns |
| **Management** | Users, Licenses, API Tokens, Settings, Webhooks |

![Clear Portal menu and Dashboard](/docs-images/clear-portal/dashboard.png)

<Callout type="warn" title="Coverage">
This guide covers the **Overview** and **Configuration** pages, plus **Licenses** and **Webhooks** from the **Management** group. The other Management pages (Users, API Tokens, Settings) will be added in a later update.
</Callout>

## Recommended setup order

If you are setting up the portal for the first time, work through the Configuration pages in this order:

<Steps>
<Step>**External System** (optional) — connect AccountWise to pick vendors and purchase orders from it. See [External System](/docs/clear-portal/external-system).</Step>
<Step>**Vendor Profiles** — add each vendor and its default wiping configuration. See [Vendor Profiles](/docs/clear-portal/vendor-profiles).</Step>
<Step>**Projects** — create a project for a vendor and purchase order. See [Project Configuration](/docs/clear-portal/project-configuration).</Step>
<Step>**Label Printer** and **Export Columns** (optional) — set up label printing and CSV columns. See [Label Printer](/docs/clear-portal/label-printer) and [Export Columns](/docs/clear-portal/export-columns).</Step>
<Step>**Webhooks** (optional) — stock devices into StockWise automatically after wiping. See [Webhooks](/docs/clear-portal/webhooks).</Step>
</Steps>
