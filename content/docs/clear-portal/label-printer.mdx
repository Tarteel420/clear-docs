The **Label Printer** page holds the connection the portal uses to print labels.

## Reviewing the connection

Click **Label Printer** under **Configuration** in the portal menu. The page shows:

- **Status** — for example, **Enabled**.
- **Printer URL** — the endpoint labels are sent to.
- **Credentials** — whether a token is stored. Stored credentials are encrypted at rest.
- **Updated** — when the connection was last changed.

![Label Printer page](/docs-images/clear-portal/label-printer.png)

## Editing the label printer

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Edit** (top right). The Edit label printer dialog opens.</Step>
<Step>
Enter the **Printer URL** — the endpoint the portal POSTs `{ serial }` to.

![Edit label printer dialog](/docs-images/clear-portal/edit-label-printer.png)
</Step>
<Step>
**Bearer token** — leave blank to keep the stored token, or enter a new value to replace it.
</Step>
<Step>Use the **Enabled** switch to turn label printing on or off.</Step>
<Step>Click **Save**.</Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnecting the label printer

Click **Disconnect** (top right) to remove the label printer connection.
